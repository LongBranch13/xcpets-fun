Rules: how every burn works

XCPETS is a Counterparty token on Bitcoin. You play by burning (destroying) XCPETS with a short text tag. Every burn is a normal Bitcoin transaction: you review and sign it in your own wallet (XCP Wallet), and you also pay a small Bitcoin network fee each time. Burned tokens are gone for good. The game state is rebuilt by anyone from the public chain: same burns, same result, no server.

Gear slots (v2)

Stats keep their original rarity bonus (Common +0, Uncommon +3, Rare +6, Legendary +10). Six slots: Headwear, Guise, Drip, Grip, Outfits, Footwear. Older, better-fed pets unlock more (1 up to 6; rarity no longer caps them), each species has one legendary signature hat (crowns are shared gear any pet can wear), and gear can be won or lost in battles (legendaries are always safe). Capacity: 1 slot, +1 at age 2, 5 and 10 days, +1 at 50% and +1 at 80% of days fed. Slots open in order Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise, Footwear.

Fewer duplicates: from block 971,588, each copy you already own makes that item 10% less likely to roll again (two copies: 81%, three: 73%, and so on), counted for your whole wallet at every roll, including rolls and roll tokens in the same burn. Rarity odds unchanged.

Stages

Egg → Hatchling (69, i.e. hatching) → Juvenile (8,349) → Adult (22,149) → Elder (41,469) → Mythic (69,069 care, reached automatically by feeding; there is no Evolve burn): a 69 hatch plus 9 meals (2 / 2 / 2 / 3 per stage), one meal a day: Juvenile day 2, Adult day 4, Elder day 6, Mythic day 9. Mythic discount (from block 971,806): once you own a Mythic pet, Mythic for all your pets needs only 21,000 care. Pets already at 21,000 or more become Mythic right away, even before Elder. Care you've already burned is kept as it is, and each of those pets gets 1 free overflow roll for every full 1,000 care above 21,000 (a 1-sat pet:of:ID burn per roll, from the pet's page).

Community pet: every burn counts toward it. It hatches at 69,000 XCPETS and grows 100× slower than a regular pet (Mythic at 6,906,900).

Looks

Eye types include Sunglasses (formerly Beady). Cheeks: Blush, None, Freckles or Tear tracks. Freckles and Tear tracks only roll for pets hatched from block 971,040 (Blush 35% / None 45% / Freckles 10% / Tear tracks 10%); older pets keep their look.

Fair play

  • Randomness comes only from Bitcoin block hashes and transaction ids.
  • Burns count in chain order. A burn that breaks a rule is never refunded, but it still feeds the community pet.
  • Burns count once they are confirmed in a block and the Counterparty node has read them.
  • Every Bitcoin transaction pays a network fee (often a few cents, more when the network is busy), so every meal costs BTC too.

⚠️ This is experimental software. Token burns are irreversible. You can lose XCPETS and Bitcoin transaction fees. No one guarantees this game will work as expected or that the token will retain any value. Use at your own risk.
