Rules: how every burn works

XCPETS is a Counterparty token on Bitcoin. You play by burning (destroying) XCPETS with a short text tag. Every burn is a normal Bitcoin transaction: you review and sign it in your own wallet (XCP Wallet), and you also pay a small Bitcoin network fee each time. Burned tokens are gone for good. The game state is rebuilt by anyone from the public chain: same burns, same result, no server.

Gear slots

Stats keep their rarity bonus (Common +0, Uncommon +3, Rare +6, Legendary +10) and are set at birth. Six slots, opened in this order: Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise, Footwear. Capacity: 1 slot, +1 at age 2, 5 and 10 days, +1 at 50% and +1 at 80% of days fed (with at least 3 fed days for the last one). Rarity doesn't change how many slots open, but it caps how rare the gear a pet rolls can be. Each species has up to 7 ★ signature pieces (6 for Dodo and Nakamoto); every species except Nakamoto has a ★ signature hat, and hybrids can wear their parents' ★ pieces except the hat. Crowns are shared gear any pet can wear. Gear can be won or lost in battles; only ★ signature pieces are never taken. A pet in the Pet Cemetery can't wear gear; its gear went back to its owner's bag.

Fewer duplicates: each copy you already own makes that item 10% less likely to roll again (two copies: 81%, three: 73%, and so on), counted for your whole wallet at every roll, including rolls and roll tokens in the same burn. Rarity odds unchanged.

Stages

Egg → Hatchling (69, i.e. hatching) → Juvenile (8,349) → Adult (22,149) → Elder (41,469) → Mythic (69,069 care, reached automatically by feeding; there is no Evolve burn): a 69 hatch plus 9 meals (2 / 2 / 2 / 3 per stage), one meal a day: Juvenile day 2, Adult day 4, Elder day 6, Mythic day 9. Mythic discount: once you own a Mythic pet, Mythic for all your pets needs only 21,000 care. Pets already at 21,000 or more become Mythic right away, even before Elder. Care you've already burned is kept as it is, and each of those pets gets 1 free overflow roll for every full 1,000 care above 21,000 (a 1-sat pet:of:ID burn per roll, from the pet's page).

Community pet: every burn counts toward it. It hatches at 69,000 XCPETS and grows 100× slower than a regular pet (Mythic at 6,906,900).

Looks

Eye types include Sunglasses (formerly Beady). Cheeks: Blush, None, Freckles or Tear tracks. Blush 35% / None 45% / Freckles 10% / Tear tracks 10%.

Fair play

  • Randomness comes only from Bitcoin block hashes and transaction ids.
  • Burns count in chain order. A burn that breaks a rule is never refunded, but it still feeds the community pet.
  • Burns count once they are confirmed in a block and the Counterparty node has read them.
  • Every Bitcoin transaction pays a network fee (often a few cents, more when the network is busy), so every meal costs BTC too.

Rule history

(when each rule switched on, by Bitcoin block)

The pages above describe the game as it works now. This is the one place that lists when each rule switched on. Every burn replays under the rule that was live at its block.

  • 971,040: one meal a day. Feeds before it followed the earlier rule (up to 3 meals a day of 1,380 / 2,300 / 3,220 / 3,450) and keep all their care. Freckles and Tear tracks cheeks roll for pets hatched from here on; older pets keep their look.
  • 971,500 / 971,501: the cutoff (every burn at or before 971,500 replays exactly like the original game). New from 971,501: battles (pet:fight:A:B 690 + pet:accept:N 690), breeding (pet:breed:A:B 690; with two owners the partner's owner accepts within 144 blocks for 690 and the egg goes to the requester) and release (pet:rel:ID / pet:rel:START:MASK, up to 120 pets per burn). Community feed: any amount of 69 or more counts (the Play page offers 69, 690 or 6,900). Every XCPETS burn by anyone counts toward the community pet. Gear: one universal pool, capacity 1-6 slots from age and care, slots Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise, Footwear.
  • 971,515: bundled feeding (pet:fm:START:MASK, a window of about 105-125 consecutive pet numbers).
  • 971,519: community gear roll (pet:c:roll, 690). 971,773: pick the slot with :HEADWEAR … :FOOTWEAR.
  • 971,525: lucky drops (25% per stage-up, 2% per meal).
  • 971,526: founder reward (gold kit plus 36 free rolls per early community gear roll, spent with pet:fr:ID).
  • 971,588: fewer duplicates (each copy you own makes that piece 10% less likely).
  • 971,632: The Return (1,111,111 burned): Nakamoto went to Cold Storage and the Bitcoin Wizard became the community pet.
  • 971,650: breed carry: the accepting pet carries the requester pet's best stat into its own future children.
  • 971,659: Nakamoto becomes a hatchable 13th species that grows and breeds like the others. 971,665: a hatch is Nakamoto exactly 1 in 777, always Legendary.
  • 971,669: each species gets a lowest tier. Pets hatched before it that were below their species' lowest tier kept their looks, moved up to that tier and gained the difference in stat bonus (for example, an Uncommon Dodo became a Legendary Dodo with +7 to every stat).
  • 971,674: two different species (Nakamoto included) give a hybrid 68.4773435% of the time; the same species gives a bred-true bonus (+3 to one stat, or a rarer pattern or eye trait).
  • 971,720: rarity no longer caps how many gear slots open.
  • 971,771: KEVIN, Sarutobi and Lenny boosted inside the Legendary tier (about 1 in 676 each, from 1 in 861).
  • 971,789: no limit on how many times a pet can breed (1,008-block wait between breeds), and hybrid parents always give a hybrid (including three-way mixes).
  • 971,806: Mythic discount (21,000 care once you own a Mythic) and overflow rolls (pet:of:ID).
  • 972,132: list feeding (pet:fl:ID.ID…): one burn feeds several pets, however far apart their numbers are.
  • 972,228: the Bitcoin Wizard's Headwear slot was cleared once, so his pointy Wizard Hat shows again (the Royal scarf stays in his collection). New community Headwear rolls still replace the hat.
