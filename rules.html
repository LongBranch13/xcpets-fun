Rules: how every burn works

XCPETS is a Counterparty token on Bitcoin. You play by burning (destroying) XCPETS with a short text tag. Every burn is a normal Bitcoin transaction: you review and sign it in your own wallet (XCP Wallet), and you also pay a small Bitcoin network fee each time. Burned tokens are gone for good. The game state is rebuilt by anyone from the public chain: same burns, same result, no server.

Dander: 1 dander = 0.00000001 XCPETS, the smallest bit of XCPETS.

Gear slots

Stats keep their rarity bonus (Common +0, Uncommon +3, Rare +6, Legendary +10) and are set at birth. Six slots, opened in this order: Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise, Footwear. Capacity: 1 slot, +1 at age 2, 5 and 10 days, +1 at 50% and +1 at 80% of days fed (with at least 3 fed days for the last one). Mythic pets have all 6 slots. Slots never close once open. Rarity doesn't change how many slots open, but it caps how rare the gear a pet rolls can be. Each species has up to 7 ★ signature pieces (6 for Dodo and Nakamoto); every species except Nakamoto has a ★ signature hat, and hybrids can wear their parents' ★ pieces except the hat. Crowns are shared gear any pet can wear. Gear can be won or lost in battles; only ★ signature pieces are never taken. A pet in the Pet Cemetery can't wear gear; its gear went back to its owner's stash.

Any-slot rolls (from block 972,646): every gear roll (paid rolls and Pepe Wheel spins, saved spins, roll tokens, free and founder rolls, overflow rolls) picks evenly from all 6 slots, however many the pet has open. A piece for a locked slot goes into that pet's bag, shown on its pet page with a 🔒 and what opens the slot. Bagged pieces belong to that pet only and are lost with it (dead or released). When the slot opens, the piece is worn if the slot is empty; gear already worn there is never replaced, and the piece goes to the stash instead.

Fewer duplicates: each copy you already own makes that item 10% less likely to roll again (two copies: 81%, three: 73%, and so on), counted for your whole wallet at every roll, including rolls and roll tokens in the same burn. Rarity odds unchanged.

Spam City Games: pet tokens (from block 972,729)

  • The token is the pet. A pet token is a free numeric Counterparty asset (1 unit, indivisible, locked, description https://xcpets.fun/t/ID.json). Making one costs no XCPETS (only the Bitcoin fee). A 1-dander burn pet:deed:ID:<asset> by the pet's owner, at least 1 block after the token is made, ties it to the pet.
  • From block 972,729, whoever holds a tied pet's token owns the pet: Counterparty sends, multi-asset (MPMA) sends, DEX fills, dispensers, any transfer. Worn and bag gear move with the pet. Only the token whose tie burn the game accepted counts; look-alike tokens with the same description are ignored.
  • Gift = send the token (🎁 Gift: pick pets, paste a Bitcoin address, Send). Several tokens go in one MPMA send (up to 10 per transaction). A pet without a token gets one made and tied first in the same flow.
  • Sell = a DEX order that gives the token for an exact amount of XCPETS (at least 1) or XCP (at least 1,000 sats), 288 blocks. Buy = the matching order. A completed match moves the pet. pet:unsell:<tx8> (1 dander) ends a listing 6 blocks later.
  • Swap = two gifts, or one token-for-token DEX order.
  • From block 972,729, pet:give, pet:gives and pet:swap burns do nothing (they are community food). Before it, nothing changed.
  • A destroyed token unties its pet (the pet stays with the last holder and can get a new token). A new token tied by the owner replaces the old one; the old one then means nothing.
  • New rules, from block 972,783: 1) Sending a token to a burn address is allowed: the pet then belongs to that burn address, is out of play for good (nobody can use it) and the site shows it as sent to a burn address. 2) One move per pet per block: only the first move of a pet's token in block order counts; later moves of it in the same block are ignored (the token still moves on Counterparty but the pet stays with the first receiver until the token moves again). 3) Making a token ties it: a token made by the pet's owner (first issuance, 1 unit, locked, description https://xcpets.fun/t/ID.json) is tied to pet #ID when it confirms, with no tie burn. Tokens made by anyone else are ignored, and a pet that already has a token keeps it (replace it with the pet:deed burn). Tie burns made before the gate by owners who still hold the token count at the gate.
  • One way to tie, from block 972,896: there is only one way to give a pet a token: make the token (first issuance by the pet's owner, 1 unit, locked, description https://xcpets.fun/t/ID.json), then tie it with a pet:deed:ID:… burn (1 dander). From this block, making a token alone does not tie it (rule 3 above ends). Pets already tied by making their token before this block stay tied.

⚡ Electric Eggs (from block 972,992)

The Electric Shock Box is one pet:hatchzap burn of 100,000 XCPETS (the halving and Fire Sales apply like every hatch, so 50,000 after the halving) that hatches 1,000 Electric Eggs. Every Electric Egg still has the same 1 in 777 chance of the special egg. Otherwise it rolls its tier on the Electric ladder below instead of the normal one. Its species is then picked from that tier exactly like a normal egg (same species odds inside each tier), and its looks are rolled the normal way.

TierNormal eggElectric EggStat bonus
Common60%30%+0
Uncommon27%40%+3
Rare10%22%+6
Legendary3%8%+10

Rare is 2.2 times as likely and Legendary 2.67 times as likely per egg. A box gives about 300 Common, 400 Uncommon, 220 Rare and 80 Legendary pets. The same XCPETS spent on Yolk Booster Boxes hatches about 2,087 eggs: about 1,252 Common, 564 Uncommon, 209 Rare and 63 Legendary. So the Electric Shock Box gives fewer pets in total, a few more Rares and about 28% more Legendaries, with far fewer Commons to feed. Mythic is a stage pets grow into by being fed, not a hatch tier, so no egg (Electric or not) hatches Mythic.

Stages

Egg → Hatchling (69, i.e. hatching) → Juvenile (8,349) → Adult (22,149) → Elder (41,469) → Mythic (69,069 care, reached automatically by feeding; there is no Evolve burn): a 69 hatch plus 9 meals (2 / 2 / 2 / 3 per stage), one meal a day: Juvenile day 2, Adult day 4, Elder day 6, Mythic day 9. Mythic shortcut: once you raise a Mythic pet yourself, Mythic for all your pets needs only 21,000 care. Raised means you hatched, bred or were granted it and it never left you, or you added more than half of its care yourself (care you add counts from the day you get the pet, and starts again at 0 if it changes owner). A Mythic you were gifted, swapped for or bought doesn't count, and neither does a pet you got close to Mythic and fed once; exactly half isn't enough. Pets already at 21,000 or more become Mythic right away, even before Elder. Care you've already burned is kept as it is, and each of those pets gets 1 free saved spin for every full 1,000 care above 21,000 (these were overflow rolls until block 972,641).

Community pet: every burn counts toward it. It hatches at 69,000 XCPETS and grows 100 times slower than a regular pet (Mythic at 6,906,900). Community pets stay community pets until they reach Mythic. Then they go into Cold Storage and become real pets anyone can hatch. The Bitcoin Wizard reached Mythic at block 972,325; Lord Kek took his place and is the community pet now. From block 972,716 player Bitcoin Wizards can breed like any pet (the community Bitcoin Wizard never breeds); player Lord Keks can too.

Saved Pepe Wheel spins

Free gear now comes as saved spins for the Pepe Wheel. You earn one from a lucky drop (25% each time a pet moves up a stage, 2% per meal), from a full-price burn during a Fire Sale, and from Mythic overflow (one per 1,000 care over 21,000). Unused founder free rolls and overflow rolls were turned into saved spins. Spins never expire. Use them in the Rare Room: one tiny burn spends up to 10 spins and costs only the Bitcoin fee. Each spin picks a random pet of yours, just like a paid spin. The Join us sign and the LORDKEK card stay special keepsakes.

The halving: burns cost half

When STAR LORD KEK defeats Lord Kek (the burn that brings Lord Kek to Mythic), Lord Kek goes to Cold Storage and, 6 blocks later, every burn costs half: hatching, meals, gear spins, fights, breeding, community rolls and community feeding (the 69 / 690 / 6,900 choices and the minimum). After that, each time a community pet goes to Cold Storage the next community pet takes its place and burns cost half again exactly once, 6 blocks after that Cold Storage block (FUNTRUMP going to Cold Storage starts halving 2). A new pet arriving adds no halving. Care never changes: a meal gives the same care as before and just costs half, so every pet grows at the same pace. Amounts can have decimals (XCPETS has 8 decimal places).

Fire Sales

Now and then a lucky block calls a Fire Sale. Captain Dan yells a warning first; 6 blocks later the sale is live for 36 blocks. During a sale every burn is 69% off (you burn 31% of the usual amount): hatching, meals, gear spins, fights, breeding, community rolls and community feeding, including the 69 / 690 / 6,900 choices and the minimum. It stacks on the halving: after a halving the halved amount is cut by 69% again. Amounts go to 8 decimal places (0.00000001 XCPETS), rounded to the nearest one, and a burn must be at least that amount.

Egg names (from block 972,897)

From block 972,897, Bitcoin Wizard, Lord Kek and FUNTRUMP eggs (and FUNTRUMP babies) get a normal random pet name, like every other egg, instead of "Wizard", "Kek" or "FUNTRUMP". The first Lord Kek egg ever hatched (#22475) keeps the name Kek, and pets hatched before this block keep their names.

Looks

Eye types include Sunglasses (formerly Beady). Cheeks: Blush, None, Freckles or Tear tracks. Blush 35% / None 45% / Freckles 10% / Tear tracks 10%.

Fair play

  • Randomness comes only from Bitcoin block hashes and transaction ids.
  • Burns count in chain order. A burn that breaks a rule is never refunded, but it still feeds the community pet.
  • Burns count once they are confirmed in a block and the Counterparty node has read them.
  • Every Bitcoin transaction pays a network fee (often a few cents, more when the network is busy), so every meal costs BTC too.

Pet looks you earn (from block 973,002)

From block 973,002 a pet can earn looks by what it does. Everything is worked out from the chain, so every player sees the same result. An earned look stays with the pet for good, also when the pet changes hands, until block 973,013; from then on it is held by the wallet (see Choose your looks). Wallet flame looks (Kindling, Wildfire, Satoshi Sun) and quest looks work exactly as before, and nobody loses a look they already have.

  • Laurels: win 10 duels while Mythic, or win 5 duels in a row (any stage; a loss starts the count again).
  • Halo: finish 10 Mythic quests (any result; quests finished before this block count).
  • Holo foil: tie the pet to a pet token (Proof of Pet). Pets already tied get it at this block.
  • Crown: be #1 on the Power board (Vigor + Spark + Grit + Zoom + Luck, ties go to the lowest pet number) at 7 daily checks in a row (7 times 144 blocks = 1,008 blocks, about a week).
  • Wings: keep a Mythic alive for 4,032 blocks (about 4 weeks), counted from this block or from the day it reached Mythic, whichever is later.
  • Flame aura: burn 69,000 XCPETS on that pet's own actions (meals, gear spins, quests and so on, from this block). The 6,900 and 690,000 milestones are recorded too.

A pet shows one look of each kind (top piece, foil, back piece, aura). Looks it already shows from wallet flame tiers or quests come first.

Flex Forge: choose your looks (from block 973,013)

From block 973,013 you decorate your pet cards: you choose which looks show on which of your pets. Every look your wallet owns can go on any pet your wallet owns: wallet flame looks (Kindling, Wildfire, Satoshi Sun), quest looks and earned looks. Use the Flex Forge on a pet page (Forge it on / Take off), the Flex Forge Station in The Rare Room, or see which card wears each look in My Flex under My Pets (Move it sends a look to another pet in one burn).

  • How: the pet's owner burns the smallest amount (0.00000001 XCPETS) with the memo pet:look:ID:CODE:on or pet:look:ID:CODE:off. One burn can carry up to 6 changes for one pet, for example pet:look:12:KL:on:QN:off. Only the owner of pet ID can change it.
  • Flame looks keep their limits: each flame look goes on 1 pet at a time (putting it on another pet moves it there), or on up to 3 pets for early burners (take it off one first to use a 4th). You need to have reached the flame tier of the look.
  • Quest and earned looks are one copy each, held by the wallet that owned the pet when it got the look. Putting a copy on another pet moves it there. A pet that earned the same look twice gives you two copies.
  • Selling or giving a pet away: all its looks come off that pet and stay with your wallet: quest and earned looks stay yours, and so do your flame looks. The new owner does not get them. You can put them on any other pet you own.
  • One look of each kind shows per pet (top piece, foil, back piece, aura and so on). Putting on a new one of the same kind takes the old one off that pet.
  • A change that fails its check (not your pet, look not owned, flame tier not reached, limit reached) is skipped; the rest of the burn still counts. If no change in the burn works, it becomes community food. Burns with this memo before block 973,013 are community food too.
  • pet:aura:ID:on and pet:aura:ID:off keep working as before.

Codes. Flame: KL Laurels, KA Flame aura, WH Halo, WR Rainbow holo, WL Lightning aura, SC Gold-foil crown, SW Holo wings, SG Galaxy skin. Quest: QN Neon trail, QA Aurora, QB Black holo, QS Spell sparkles, QG Gold holo. Earned: EL Laurels, EH Halo, EF Holo foil, EC Crown, EW Wings, EA Flame aura.

🐉 Raids (from block 973,010)

Every 1,008 blocks (about a week) a Raid Boss appears, starting at block 973,010. Each raid lasts 432 blocks (about 3 days). Everyone burns together to bring it down. Everything is worked out from the chain, so every player sees the same result.

  • Which boss: the hash of the block that opens the raid picks one of 13 bosses. When a raid window holds a block whose height ends in 2140, the event boss Lord Blockschild shows up instead.
  • Boss HP: fixed for each boss: The Night Shift Grinner 420,000; Night King 600,000; Grim Reaper 666,000; Baron Von Burnschild, The All-Seeing Kek and Necrofrog the Undying 690,000; The Grinning Colossus 750,000; Infernal Frogfather 777,000; Dragonkek 800,000; Dark Pepe the Endless Coil 840,000; Moloch 900,000 (Lord Kek vs Moloch: every hit helps Lord Kek strike him); The Tower of Terrors 999,000; Titan 1,000,000; Lord Blockschild (event) 2,140,000.
  • Attack: burn at least 69 XCPETS (the halving and Fire Sales apply) with the memo pet:raid:ID from the pet's owner. The pet must be hatched, alive, not neglected, not away on a quest and not in Cold Storage. One attack per pet per chain day (144 blocks).
  • Damage = burn amount times the pet multiplier times the crit roll. The pet multiplier is 1.00 plus a stage bonus (Hatchling 0, Juvenile 0.10, Adult 0.20, Elder 0.30, Mythic 0.40) plus a rarity bonus (Common 0, Uncommon 0.03, Rare 0.06, Legendary 0.10), so from 1.00 up to 1.50. The crit roll comes from the burn's transaction id: 3 times (mega crit) 5% of the time, 2 times (crit) 20% of the time, otherwise 1 time. Damage is rounded down.
  • Killing blow: the attack that takes the boss to 0 HP wins the raid; damage past 0 does not count. After that, attacks on that raid are turned down (community food). If the boss still has HP when the raid ends, it escapes and nobody gets a reward.
  • Rewards (only when the boss falls): every pet that attacked gets the RAIDER badge; the top 3 pets by damage get the 🥇 🥈 🥉 trophies (ties go to the pet that hit first); the pet that landed the killing blow gets the 🗡️ Killing Blow trophy; and one lucky drop (a 🍀 Boss Relic plus 3 saved Pepe Wheel spins for its owner) goes to one attacking pet, picked by the hash of the kill block, with odds in line with the damage each pet dealt. There is no Gold holo reward.
  • Confirmations: results count 6 blocks after the kill (or after the raid ends), so a short chain reorg cannot change them.
