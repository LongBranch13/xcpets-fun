Set up your Pet Shell

Your pet, live, in your hand. Four steps, one USB-C cable, about five minutes. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

🔒 No keys inside

The Pet Shell only looks: it reads your pet from the public game files. It never holds a wallet key, never signs and never sends anything. Feeding shows a QR code: scan it with your phone and your own wallet signs the burn on xcpets.fun, with all the usual checks. This page never asks for a seed phrase or private key. Never type one anywhere.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer.

1 · Put the software on

  1. Plug the Pet Shell into this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data (some cheap cables only charge).
  2. Click the button and pick the Pet Shell in the list (often called USB JTAG/serial debug unit).
  3. Click Install. It takes about a minute.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.This page must be opened over https.

Stuck on "Failed to connect"? Hold the BOOT button on the board, tap RST, let go of BOOT, then try again. Already installed? Go straight to step 2.

2 · Pick your pet

Paste your wallet address (public, starts with bc1, 1 or 3). Then tap the pet that should live in this Pet Shell.

3 · Wi-Fi

The Pet Shell checks on your pet over Wi-Fi every 10 minutes. It needs a 2.4 GHz network (most home Wi-Fi has one). The password is stored only on the Pet Shell.

4 · Send to my Pet Shell

Keep it plugged in. Click the button, pick the Pet Shell in the list, and wait for the 🎉.

Using it

  • Press either button: next screen (your pet → feed QR → info).
  • Hold a button for 1.5 seconds: check for news now.
  • Hungry? Open the feed QR, scan it with your phone, then sign it in your wallet on xcpets.fun.
  • On battery it dims after a minute and sleeps after five. Press KEY to wake it.
  • New pet or new Wi-Fi? Come back here and do steps 2 to 4 again.
