How It Works

XCPETS is a burn-to-play pet game on Bitcoin. Every action is a real burn (a Counterparty destroy) of XCPETS that you sign in your own XCP Wallet. There's no server and no account. The game rebuilds everyone's pets from the public burn history, so every player sees the same result. The Rules page has the exact tags, odds and edge cases.

1. Getting started

  1. Install the XCP Wallet extension. It runs in desktop Chrome only.
  2. Get some XCPETS on xcp.fun, plus a little BTC for network fees, into that wallet.
  3. Press Connect XCP Wallet on Play. Before every burn the game shows the amount, the tag and the Bitcoin fee, then XCP Wallet asks for your final approval. Nothing burns unless you approve both. Disconnect forgets the address on this site.
  4. Hatch an egg, and feed it the same day.
  5. Feed it every day. Roll gear once slots open. Battle from Juvenile, breed from Adult.

3. Hatch eggs

Hatch one egg, a dozen or a case. A pack is a single burn, so it's cheaper per egg and you pay one Bitcoin fee instead of many. The Bitcoin block your burn lands in decides each pet's species, rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare or Legendary) and looks, so nobody can pick or predict them. Rarer species only hatch at rarer tiers; Nakamotos are no longer hatched; existing ones are kept. The 1 in 777 egg is now a Bitcoin Wizard egg. When Lord Kek reaches Mythic and goes to Cold Storage, Wizard eggs stop and that 1 in 777 egg becomes a Lord Kek egg. There is no other way to get either one. The odds are in the Pet Guide. After the burn confirms, a reveal shows your new pets.

4. Feed and grow to Mythic

  • A hatched pet starts as a Hatchling. Bred babies start as an Egg and need a 69 XCPETS meal to hatch.
  • Only the pet's owner can feed it, once a day (a day is 144 Bitcoin blocks).
  • Each feed costs that stage's meal: 4,140 (Hatchling), 6,900 (Juvenile), 9,660 (Adult), 9,200 (Elder). Burn less and the feed doesn't count; burn more and the extra only feeds the community pet. The Play page fills in the exact amount.
  • Care moves the pet through Hatchling → Juvenile → Adult → Elder → Mythic. Fed every day, a pet turns Mythic on its own after 9 meals (about 9 days, 69,069 care).
  • Feeding on consecutive days builds your pet's feed streak (and badges). Your WAGMI streak is separate: see Rankings.
  • You can feed several of your pets in one burn, even when their numbers are far apart. Feed all hungry and Feed selected on Play set it up for you.
  • Mythic discount: once you own a Mythic, your other pets only need 21,000 care to turn Mythic. A pet already past 21,000 then earns one free saved Pepe Wheel spin for every full 1,000 care above 21,000.

5. Feed daily or it dies

  • From the 3rd day after its last meal, a pet is neglected: it can't battle or breed.
  • After 7 full days with no meal, it dies. The clock starts at hatch if a pet was never fed.
  • A dead pet moves to the Pet Cemetery. Its gear goes back to the owner's stash.
  • Mythic pets never starve.
  • Play and each pet's page warn you when a pet is close to starving.

6. Gear

  • Pets wear gear in six slots, which open in this order: Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise, Footwear. A new pet has 1 open; more open as it gets older and stays fed (up to 6). Mythic pets have all 6 slots. Slots never close once open. Rarity doesn't change how many slots open, but it does cap how rare the gear it rolls can be.
  • A gear roll (345) is a Pepe Wheel spin: it picks one of your living pets at random (rarer pets are picked more often: Common 1, Uncommon 2, Rare 4, Legendary 8) and puts the new piece straight on that pet.
  • From block 972,646 a roll can land in any of the 6 slots, even ones the pet hasn't opened. Those pieces wait in the pet's bag 🎒 (shown on its page with a 🔒 and what opens the slot) and are worn as soon as the slot opens, unless something is already worn there. The bag belongs to that pet and is lost with it.
  • Lucky drops 🎟️: every stage-up has a 25% chance, and every other meal a 2% chance, to drop one free saved Pepe Wheel spin. Use saved spins from the Rare Room, up to 10 in one tiny burn.
  • Pieces you already own are a little less likely to roll again.
  • Every species except Nakamoto has a ★ signature hat, plus a few other ★ pieces only it can wear (hybrids can wear their parents' ★ pieces, except the hat). Crowns are shared gear any pet can wear.
  • Open a pet's Gear to take pieces off or put them on from your stash or another of your pets. Equipping is free and only saved in this browser. Other people see your pet's gear as it was rolled, not your changes.
  • A pet in the Pet Cemetery can't wear gear.

7. X Circus Kekimus (fights)

  • Fights happen in X Circus Kekimus on Play, in one Challenge card: pick both pets with the picker filters, compare, then burn. Challenge for 690; the other owner accepts for 690 within 144 blocks.
  • Pets must be Juvenile or older and not neglected; one fight per pet per day. The loser gives up one random worn piece (★ pieces never) and gets a black eye for 72 blocks.
  • Title belts are just for show: a pet gets one at 3 wins in a row, and it goes back into the trophy case when the pet loses. No betting.

8. Breeding

  • Two Adult (or older) pets can breed for 135. If the partner belongs to someone else, its owner accepts within 144 blocks for another 135, and the egg goes to you.
  • Every pet can breed 6 times in its life, with a one-week wait (1,008 blocks) between breeds. No breeding with a parent, child or sibling.
  • Hybrids also inherit body parts from the other parent, and there are rare twins, mutations and hidden traits.
  • Two different species often give a hybrid; the same species gives a bonus trait.
  • The baby is an Egg. Feed it 69 XCPETS to hatch it. Its hunger clock starts right away.

Breeding odds in the Pet Guide →

9. Release vs Discard

  • Discard is free: no burn, no wallet. It only hides the pet in this browser. The pet stays on chain, and you can bring it back any time with Show discarded → Restore. Use Discard on single pets, Discard all Commons or Discard selected.
  • Release is a tiny on-chain burn (0.00000001) that gives the pet up for good, for everyone. Its gear goes back to your stash. It can't be undone.

10. The community pet: Lord Kek

  • Every XCPETS burn by anyone also feeds the community pet, Lord Kek. He took the Bitcoin Wizard's place when the Wizard reached Mythic and went to Cold Storage.
  • He grows 100 times slower than a normal pet and never starves: he hatched at 69,000 burned and turns Mythic at 6,906,900. Watch it grow on the home page.
  • Feed it directly with 69, 690 or 6,900 on Play, or burn 690 for a community gear roll. It always wears the newest piece.
  • Community pets stay community pets until they reach Mythic. Then they go into Cold Storage and become real pets anyone can hatch. Player Bitcoin Wizards come only from the 1 in 777 egg. From block 972,716 they breed like any pet (135 per breed); the community Bitcoin Wizard in Cold Storage never breeds. When Lord Kek reaches Mythic, that egg hatches Lord Keks instead, and player Lord Keks can breed.
  • Before the Wizard, Nakamoto was the community pet until The Return, when the community burned 1,111,111. His story is in Cold Storage.

12. Bitcoin fees

Every burn is a Bitcoin transaction, so it costs a small BTC network fee on top of the XCPETS. The game picks the lowest fee rate that should still confirm within about an hour and shows the fee before you approve. A burn takes effect once it's confirmed in a block. Until then it shows under Your burns as pending, and you can tap any burn to see its status and explorer links.
