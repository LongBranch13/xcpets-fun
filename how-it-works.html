    How It Works

    🎓 New here? Take the The Big Beautiful Tutorial: a short guided tour with practice demos. Or let Coach Peepo show you around the Play page.

    XCPETS is a burn-to-play pet game on Bitcoin. Every action is a real burn (a Counterparty destroy) of XCPETS that you sign in your own XCP Wallet. There's no server and no account. The game rebuilds everyone's pets from the public burn history, so every player sees the same result. The Rules page has the exact tags, odds and edge cases. Don't take our word for it: 🔎 Verify it yourself shows any pet's burn, block hash and seed, and replays the whole game in your browser.

    1. Getting started

    1. Install the XCP Wallet extension on desktop Chrome. Play on your phone: open xcpets.fun inside the Casa Tookan app, which signs burns the same way.
    2. Get some XCPETS on xcp.fun, plus a little BTC for network fees, into that wallet.
    3. Press Connect XCP Wallet on Play. Before every burn the game shows the amount, the tag and the Bitcoin fee, then XCP Wallet asks for your final approval. Nothing burns unless you approve both. Disconnect forgets the address on this site.
    4. Hatch an egg, and feed it the same day.
    5. Feed it every day. Roll gear for any of its 6 slots. Battle from Juvenile, breed from Adult.

    3. Hatch eggs

    Crack One Open, a Crack Pack (12) or a Yolk Booster Box (144). From block 972,992 the ⚡ Electric Shock Box hatches 1,000 Electric Eggs in one burn with boosted odds (Legendary 8%, Rare 22%, Uncommon 40%, Common 30%; details). A pack is a single burn, so it's cheaper per egg and you pay one Bitcoin fee instead of many. The Bitcoin block your burn lands in decides each pet's species, rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare or Legendary) and looks, so nobody can pick or predict them. Rarer species only hatch at rarer tiers; Nakamotos are no longer hatched; existing ones are kept. The 1 in 777 egg is now a FUNTRUMP egg. When Homer Pepe reaches Mythic and goes to Cold Storage, FUNTRUMP eggs stop and that 1 in 777 egg becomes a Homer Pepe egg. There is no other way to get either one. The odds are in the Pet Guide. After the burn confirms, a reveal shows your new pets.

    4. Feed and grow to Mythic

    • A hatched pet starts as a Hatchling. Bred babies start as an Egg and need a 17.25 XCPETS meal to hatch.
    • Only the pet's owner can feed it, once a day (a day is 144 Bitcoin blocks).
    • Each feed costs that stage's meal: 1,035 (Hatchling), 1,725 (Juvenile), 2,415 (Adult), 2,300 (Elder). Burn less and the feed doesn't count; burn more and the extra only feeds the community pet. The Play page fills in the exact amount.
    • Care moves the pet through Hatchling → Juvenile → Adult → Elder → Mythic. Fed every day, a pet turns Mythic on its own after 9 meals (about 9 days, 69,069 care).
    • Feeding on consecutive days builds your pet's feed streak (and badges). Your WAGMI streak is separate: see Rankings.
    • Feed all hungry on Play feeds the hungry pets you own that can still eat. It is usually one burn, pet:fr, for the exact total of their meals. Mythic pets do not eat, and a pet away on a quest is left out. If the herd does not fit in one tag, it splits into as few pet:fr burns as needed. One pet is pet:feed:ID. Pets whose numbers sit close together can share a pet:fm window when that is fewer burns. Feed selected does the same for the pets you tick. The old one-burn tag pet:fa was retired at block 974,163: a pet:fa burn now only feeds the community pet, and the feed-skip list is retired with it. Discarded pets are left out of the feeds the site plans. Details in the Rules.
    • Mythic shortcut: once you raise a Mythic yourself, your other pets only need 21,000 care to turn Mythic. Raised means you hatched, bred or were granted it and it never left you, or you fed it more than half of its care yourself. A Mythic you were gifted, swapped for or bought (shop or token) doesn't count, and neither does a pet you got close to Mythic and fed once or twice; exactly half isn't enough. A pet already past 21,000 then earns one free saved Pepe Wheel spin for every full 1,000 care above 21,000.

    5. Feed daily or it dies

    • From the 3rd day after its last meal, a pet is neglected: it can't battle or breed.
    • After 7 full days with no meal, it dies. The clock starts at hatch if a pet was never fed.
    • From block 977,026, a pet that has been dead for 14 game days leaves the game. A game day is 144 blocks. The pause from block 975,817 to 976,518 does not count. Legendary and Mythic pets stay. So do rare species (the limited ones, not a normal Pepe and not just a Rare tier), hybrids, special pets (1 of 1s, contest prizes, burn lords, grants, tokenized pets, jackpot pets, and Legend hatches), and weird hatches (tiny, giant, two-headed, inverted, ghost, backwards, and wrong egg). An Electric Egg is not spared for being an Electric Egg. A burn aimed at a pet that has left only feeds the community pet. The full list is in the Rules.
    • A dead pet moves to the Pet Cemetery. Its gear goes back to the owner's stash.
    • Mythic pets never starve.
    • Play and each pet's page warn you when a pet is close to starving.

    6. Gear

    • Pets wear gear in six slots: Headwear, Grip, Armor, Drip, Guise, Footwear. From block 973,560 every pet has all 6 gear slots open, whatever its age, feeding, tier or stage (new pets too). (Before that block, a new pet had 1 open and more opened as it got older and stayed fed.) Rarity doesn't change how many slots a pet has, but it does cap how rare the gear it rolls can be.
    • A gear roll (86.25) is a Pepe Wheel spin: it picks one of your living pets at random (rarer pets are picked more often: Common 1, Uncommon 2, Rare 4, Legendary 8) and puts the new piece straight on that pet.
    • From block 972,646 a roll can land in any of the 6 slots. Before block 973,560, pieces for a slot the pet hadn't opened waited in its bag 🎒; at that block every slot opens and bagged pieces are worn (or go to the stash if that slot is already filled). The bag belongs to that pet and is lost with it.
    • Lucky drops 🎟️: every stage-up has a 25% chance, and every other meal a 2% chance, to drop one free saved Pepe Wheel spin. Use saved spins from the Rare Room, up to 10 in one tiny burn.
    • Pieces you already own are a little less likely to roll again.
    • Every species except Nakamoto has a ★ signature hat, plus a few other ★ pieces only it can wear (hybrids can wear their parents' ★ pieces, except the hat). Crowns are shared gear any pet can wear.
    • Open a pet's Gear to take pieces off or put them on from your stash or another of your pets. Equipping is free and only saved in this browser. Other people see your pet's gear as it was rolled, not your changes.
    • A pet in the Pet Cemetery can't wear gear.

    7. X Circus Kekimus (fights)

    • Fights happen in X Circus Kekimus on Play, in one Challenge card: pick both pets with the picker filters, compare, then burn. Challenge for 172.5; the other owner accepts for 172.5 within 144 blocks.
    • Pets must be Juvenile or older and not neglected; one fight per pet per day. The loser gives up one random worn piece (★ pieces never) and gets a black eye for 72 blocks.
    • Title belts are just for show: a pet gets one at 3 wins in a row, and it goes back into the trophy case when the pet loses. No betting.

    8. Breeding

    • Two Adult (or older) pets can breed for 33.75. If the partner belongs to someone else, its owner accepts within 144 blocks for another 33.75, and the egg goes to you. The first confirmed request gets the egg. If you both check in, whoever confirms first is the requester. The other burn, with the pets flipped, is the accept: that owner does not get the egg, and their pet carries the requester's best stat.
    • Every pet can breed 6 times in its life, with a one-week wait (1,008 blocks) between breeds. No breeding with a parent, child or sibling.
    • Hybrids also inherit body parts from the other parent, and there are rare twins, mutations and hidden traits.
    • Two different species often give a hybrid; the same species gives a bonus trait.
    • The baby is an Egg. Feed it 17.25 XCPETS to hatch it. Its hunger clock starts right away.

    9. Release vs Discard

    • Discard is one tiny on-chain burn (1 dander plus the Bitcoin fee, however many pets it names). The pets stay yours, but they leave your feeds, the cemetery place lists and the Pepe Wheel, and they can't breed. Show discarded → Restore adds them back with the same tiny burn. If you cancel the burn, the pet is only hidden in this browser. Use Discard on single pets, Discard all Commons, Discard selected or 🧹 Clear the pile.
    • Release (pet:rel) was retired at block 974,163: such a burn now only feeds the community pet. Use Discard.

    10. The community pet: Homer Pepe

    • Every XCPETS burn by anyone also feeds the community pet, Homer Pepe. He took FUNTRUMP's place when FUNTRUMP reached Mythic and went to Cold Storage.
    • Feed it directly with 17.25, 172.5 or 1,725 on Play, or burn 172.5 for a community gear roll. At Mythic it goes into Cold Storage and the next community pet takes its place.
    • Player FUNTRUMPs come from the 1 in 777 egg since FUNTRUMP went to Cold Storage (block 973,365). When Homer Pepe reaches Mythic, that egg hatches Homer Pepes instead, and player Homer Pepes can breed.

    11. Rankings

    The Ranking page has 8 boards. WAGMI → NGMI is first: daily WAGMI check-in streaks per wallet (burn 1 dander with pet:wagmi once a chain day; Play offers it once right after you connect). Today's first five shows the first 5 wallets to say WAGMI this chain day: each gets +1 saved Pepe Wheel spin and the DAY ONE DEGEN badge. The others rank the most pets, power, rarest collection, top burners, battle wins, Legendary hunters and oldest pets. Below them, NGMI lists wallets with living pets that haven't said WAGMI yet this chain day, biggest streak at risk first.

    Spam City Games: sell, buy, swap and gift pets

    • Open 🛒 Spam City. From block 972,729 each pet can have a pet token: a Counterparty asset tied to it. Whoever holds the token owns the pet, wherever the token goes.
    • Sell: make the token (free, Bitcoin fee only), wait 1 block, tie it (1 dander), then list it on the Counterparty DEX for XCPETS or XCP. Buy: one wallet window; the pet and its gear move when the match confirms.
    • Gift: pick pets, paste the friend's Bitcoin address (bc1…, 1… or 3…), Send. That is a token send (several pets = one send). Untokened pets get a token first, automatically.
    • Swap: gift each other, or place a token-for-token DEX order.
    • Check a token: the pet page and xcp.io show it; only the token the game tied counts.
    • From block 972,783: only a pet's first move in a block counts, and tokens sent to a burn address put the pet out of play. Details.
    • One way to tie, from block 972,896: make the token, then tie it with a 1-dander burn. Making a token alone no longer ties it. Pets already tied by making their token before that block stay tied.

    12. Bitcoin fees

    Every burn is a Bitcoin transaction, so it costs a small BTC network fee on top of the XCPETS. The game picks the lowest fee rate that should still confirm within about an hour and shows the fee before you approve. A burn takes effect once it's confirmed in a block. Until then it shows under Your burns as pending, and you can tap any burn to see its status and explorer links.
