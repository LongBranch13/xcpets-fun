XCPETS: How It Works

XCPETS is a burn-to-play pet game on Bitcoin. Every action is a real burn (a Counterparty destroy) of XCPETS that you sign in your own XCP Wallet. There's no server and no account. The game rebuilds everyone's pets from the public burn history, so every player sees the same result. This page is a short guide. The Rules page has the exact prices, burn tags, odds and edge cases.

1. Connect your wallet

Install the XCP Wallet Chrome extension, then press Connect wallet. Before every burn, the game shows a confirm box with the amount, the tag and the Bitcoin fee. Then XCP Wallet asks for your final approval. Nothing is burned unless you approve both. Disconnect forgets the address on this site.

2. Hatch eggs

Hatch one egg, a dozen (12) or a case (144). A pack is a single burn, so it's cheaper per egg and you pay one Bitcoin fee instead of many. The Bitcoin block your burn lands in decides each pet's species, rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare or Legendary) and looks, so nobody can pick or predict them. From block 971,669 the species depends on the tier: Pepe, Wojak, Chud, Peepo and Doge can hatch at any tier; Bobo, Mumu, Sarutobi and Lenny need Uncommon or better; Snibby and KEVIN need Rare or better; Dodo is Legendary only; and each species is four times as likely in the tier where it unlocks. Nakamoto is 1 in 777 eggs and always Legendary. Existing pets are relabeled at that block: a pet whose species is below its lowest allowed tier keeps its species and looks, moves up to that tier and gets the extra stat bonus (+3 Uncommon, +6 Rare, +10 Legendary, minus what it had). Per-tier odds are in the Pet Guide. After the burn confirms, a reveal shows your new pets.

3. Feed and grow to Mythic

Each meal is a burn tagged for that pet, and only the pet's owner can feed it. A meal adds at most the current stage's meal size, and a hatch gives at most 69 care, so burning extra doesn't skip anything. You can feed a pet once a day (a day is 144 Bitcoin blocks): one big meal, so Mythic still comes on day 9. Meals get bigger at higher stages. Care moves the pet through Egg → Hatchling → Juvenile → Adult → Elder. Feeding on consecutive days builds a streak. Once an Elder is fully fed (69,069 care), it turns Mythic on its own. There is no extra Evolve burn.

Bundled feeding: from block 971,515, one burn feeds your pets within a window of about 105-125 consecutive pet numbers (pets spread far apart need extra burns) (tag pet:fm:START:MASK). You pay the exact total of their meals; pets are fed in ID order, and any pet the burn runs short for is skipped, not partly fed. Feed all hungry on the play page bundles automatically, so a big wallet needs a handful of burns instead of one per pet.

Feed several pets in one burn: you can now feed several of your pets in one burn, even when their numbers are far apart. Feed all hungry and Feed selected on the play page set it up for you.

4. Gear

Each pet has six gear slots: Headwear, Guise, Drip, Grip, Outfits, Footwear. Older, better-fed pets unlock more (1 up to 6; rarity no longer caps them), each species has one legendary signature hat (crowns are shared gear any pet can wear), and gear can be won or lost in battles (legendaries are always safe). A gear roll burn picks an item for one of the pet's open slots. New gear goes to the pet's bag; with Auto-equip new gear on (saved per device) it is worn right away, never swapping in a weaker piece. Your rolled items appear in Inventory. Open a pet's Gear to take items off or equip them from your inventory or another of your pets (with a preview). Equipping is free and saved on this device; other viewers see the pet's rolled gear.

Roll tokens 🎟️: From block 971,525, every time a pet moves up a stage (hatching counts, including each egg of a dozen or a case) it has a 25% chance of a roll token, and every meal without a stage-up a 2% chance (each pet fed by a bundled feed gets its own chance; at most one token per pet per burn). The token turns into one free gear item right away, with no burn: it is rolled exactly like a paid gear roll, into a random open slot, from the block hash and transaction of the burn that earned it plus the pet's id, so everyone replaying the chain gets the same item. Like any new gear it goes to the pet's bag, or is worn with Auto-equip new gear on (never a weaker piece), and it shows in the pet's history; the inventory counts the tokens your pets found. Slots open as a pet gets older and better fed.

Fewer duplicates: from block 971,588, each copy you already own makes that item 10% less likely to roll again (two copies: 81%, three: 73%, and so on), counted for your whole wallet at every roll, including rolls and roll tokens in the same burn. Rarity odds unchanged.

5. Discard pets

Don't want some pets? On the review screen tick Discard on single pets, or use Discard all Commons or Discard selected; you can also discard from a pet's detail view, its pet page, or Delete all Commons in My Pets. Discarding is free for every rarity: no burn and no wallet. It only hides the pet in your browser. The pet stays on chain, and you can bring it back any time with Show discarded → Restore.

6. The community pet

Every XCPETS burn by any player also feeds one shared community pet. It grows through the same stages as the total burned climbs, and its name grows with it. Players can also feed it directly or burn for its gear.

Community gear roll: from block 971,519, a 690 XCPETS burn tagged pet:c:roll gives the community pet one random gear item on the spot (from block 971,773, add :HEADWEAR, :GRIP, :OUTFITS, :DRIP, :GUISE or :FOOTWEAR to pick the slot). The Bitcoin Wizard, the community pet, always wears it right away (the newest piece replaces that slot), and everyone sees the same gear. The Community gear roll button on the play page builds the burn for you.

Founder reward: the two wallets behind the early 25,000 community gear rolls get one gold kit plus 36 free gear rolls per community gear roll, from block 971,526. They spend a free roll with a tiny pet:fr:ID burn; the play page shows a Free roll button and how many are left.

7. WAGMI → NGMI ranking

The Ranking page sorts every pet on the chain from WAGMI (best cared for) to NGMI, using stage, care and streak.

8. Bitcoin fees

Every burn is a Bitcoin transaction, so it costs a small BTC network fee on top of the XCPETS. The game picks the lowest fee rate that should still confirm within about an hour and shows the fee before you approve. A burn takes effect once it's confirmed in a block. Until then it shows under Your burns as pending, and you can tap any burn to see its status and explorer links.

9. Risk

This is experimental software. Burns are final and can't be undone or refunded. You can lose XCPETS and Bitcoin fees, and nobody guarantees the game or the token will keep any value. Only burn what you're fine losing.
