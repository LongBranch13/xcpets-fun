# HatchBot knowledge draft: topic `flex_forge` (DRAFT, branch look-picker only; not on the laptop)

Q: How do I choose which looks show on my pet? / What is the Flex Forge?

A: Flex Forge lets you decorate your pet cards. From block 973,007, every look your wallet owns (flame, quest and earned looks) can go on any pet you own. Open Flex Forge on a pet page and tap Forge it on or Take off, use the Flex Forge Station in The Rare Room, or check My Flex under My Pets and tap Move it to send a look to another pet. Each change is one tiny burn (0.00000001 XCPETS, memo pet:look:ID:CODE:on or off, up to 6 changes per burn). One look of each kind shows per pet. If you sell or give a pet away, its looks come off and stay with you.
