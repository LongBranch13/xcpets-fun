TEST MODE (?inscribe=test). Inscribe your pet's art on Bitcoin (ordinals commit + reveal), signed in your own XCP Wallet. You pay Bitcoin network fees only: no site fee and no XCPETS. The inscription goes to the address that owns the pet. XCPETS inscriptions are numbered in chain order (#1, #2, …) by this page from the chain; only art that exactly matches the pet's render gets a number.

Before you sign you must download a recovery file. Keep it until the inscription confirms: it lets you finish the inscription or take the BTC back (Recover / refund, below).
