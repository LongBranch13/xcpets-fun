Petcoin: A Peer-to-Pet Electronic Pet System
Miwa #15004
Nakamoto, Mythic · xcpets.fun/pet.html?id=15004

Abstract. A purely peer-to-pet version of electronic pets would allow a pet to be fed directly by its keeper without going through a petting zoo. Burns provide part of the solution, but the main benefits are lost if a trusted zookeeper is still required to prevent double-feeding. We propose a solution to the double-feeding problem using a peer-to-pet network. The network timestamps meals by burning them into an ongoing chain of proof-of-care, forming a record that cannot be changed without redoing all the care. The longest chain not only serves as proof of the sequence of snacks witnessed, but proof that it came from the largest pool of care. As long as a majority of care comes from honest keepers, they will grow the longest chain and outpace any goblin. The network itself requires minimal structure. Pets can leave and rejoin at will, accepting the longest proof-of-care chain as proof of what happened while they were napping.

1. Introduction

Pets on the Internet have come to rely almost exclusively on servers serving as trusted third parties. Servers go down, games shut off, and pets vanish like ghosts at sunrise. What is needed is a pet system based on cryptographic proof instead of trust, so that any keeper can check any pet, at any block, by replaying the chain.

2. Treats

We define an electronic pet as a chain of burns. Each keeper feeds the pet by burning a treat on chain. A reader can verify the chain of ownership and care without asking anyone, the same way you can count the candles on a cake.

3. Timestamp Server

Every hatch, meal and battle is stamped by a Bitcoin block. Each stamp includes the previous one, forming a chain, with each additional block reinforcing the ones before it. The Cemetery never forgets: a pet left unfed for 7 chain days is written into the graveyard for all to see.

4. Proof-of-Care

Care is counted, not claimed. A pet's stage, its gear and its ⚡ power all fall out of the replay. To change the past, a goblin would have to redo the care of every block after it and then catch up with honest keepers, which gets harder with every block, like outrunning a full moon.

5. Privacy

Keepers are known only by their addresses. Pets wear their names proudly. Bats see everything but say nothing.

6. Conclusion

We have proposed a system for electronic pets without relying on trust. Born on Halloween, eighteen years after the paper that started it all. Happy birthday, white paper. 🎃

Inspired by “Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System”, Satoshi Nakamoto, Oct 31, 2008.

How a grant to every pet could work (for the owner to decide)
  • Slot: a keepsake outside the 6 gear slots (head, hand, body, back, face, feet), so no pet has to take anything off. Drawn as a scroll in the paw on the pet card only when the hand slot is empty.
  • Stats: cosmetic only. No fight damage, no stat change, ⚡ power stays the same.
  • Who gets it: every pet alive at one activation block (picked by the owner), counted by the replay, so every reader of the chain agrees. Pets hatched later could get it until Nov 1, 2026 if the owner wants.
  • Gate: free, no burn, cannot be traded or rolled. Miwa #15004 gets the “author's copy”. Nothing happens until the owner picks the block and the replay check passes.
