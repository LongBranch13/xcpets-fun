# Old Hickory Pepe (1 of 1 for GMONEY)

Name ideas: Old Hickory Pepe (main pick), Pepe Jackson, Hickory Kek, General Pepe, The Portrait Pepe.

Story: For years he hung in a gold frame on the office wall and watched every meeting with the same stern look. Nobody could stare him down.
One night the frame creaked, a puff of white hair leaned out, and a very serious frog stepped down onto the carpet, still in his rust cloak and high
white collar. He is tough as hickory, he never blinks first (well, only at idle frame 2), and he only answers to GMONEY. Feed him and he grows
from a cream egg into an old general with a hickory cane and, at Mythic, a small silver medal he says he won for "outstaring a wall".
(A playful pet made in the style of a meme portrait. It makes no claims about any real person.)

Stages: Egg (cream shell, white hair swirl, rust band) / Hatchling / Juvenile / Adult / Elder (greyer hair + hickory cane) / Mythic (medal on a red ribbon).
4 idle frames each: bob 0,-1,-1,0, blink on frame 2. No fire aura, no flame crown, no gold frame.
