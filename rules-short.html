Rules in short

Short version. The full page with every detail stays at Rules; every date and old rule is in Rule history.

XCPETS is a Counterparty token on Bitcoin. You play by burning XCPETS with a short tag. Each burn is a Bitcoin transaction you sign in your own wallet; it also pays a small Bitcoin network fee (in sats). Anyone can rebuild the game from the chain: same burns, same result, no server.

Dander: 1 dander = 0.00000001 XCPETS. Costs below are today’s (after 2 halvings). A Fire Sale cuts every cost to 31% for 36 blocks.

1. Hatch

  • pet:hatch 17.25 · one egg. pet:hatch12 172.5 · 12 eggs. pet:hatch144 1,725 · 144 eggs. pet:hatchzap 25,000 · 1,000 Electric Eggs (better tier odds).
  • Species, tier and looks come from the block hash and your tx id, so nobody knows them in advance. Paying less hatches nothing (it feeds the community pet).

2. Daily care

  • One meal a day per pet (a day = 144 blocks). Meal: 1,035 / 1,725 / 2,415 / 2,300 by stage. Only the owner can feed; Mythics don’t eat.
  • Feed all is one burn: pet:fr:AA:RUNS, the exact total of the meals. Single pet: pet:feed:ID.
  • WAGMI: pet:wagmi, 1 dander, once a day keeps your 🔥 streak. The first 5 wallets each day get +1 saved spin.
  • 🧹 Daily chores (new, from block 974,163): pet:dc:F[:AA:RUNS] does WAGMI + Feed all + use all saved spins in one burn. Cost = the meals (at least 1 dander). Each part is skipped on its own if there is nothing to do.
  • Stages: Hatchling → Juvenile (day 2) → Adult (day 4) → Elder (day 6) → Mythic (day 9). Once you raise a Mythic yourself, your other pets reach Mythic at 21,000 care.

3. Gear and the Pepe Wheel

  • Every pet has 6 open slots: Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise, Footwear.
  • pet:spin 86.25 · picks one of your pets (Legendary 8 times as likely as Common) and puts a new piece on it.
  • Saved spins come from lucky drops (25% per stage-up, 2% per meal), Fire Sales, Mythic overflow and raids. From block 974,279 they land only on pets you pick: Pick pets for your spins in the Rare Room (pet:ut, only the Bitcoin fee). Before that block each spin went to a random living pet of yours, never-fed and discarded pets included.
  • Looks: pet:look:ID:CODE:on|off, 1 dander, up to 6 changes per burn.

4. Fight, breed, raid, quest

  • Fight: pet:fight:A:B, accepted with pet:accept:N. The loser can lose one worn piece (★ signature pieces are never taken).
  • Breed: pet:breed:A:B 135 (Adult or older, 6 breeds per pet, 1,008 blocks between breeds). Two owners: both pay 135.
  • Raid: pet:raid:ID, 17.25 or more, once per pet per day. Damage = amount times pet bonus (1.00 to 1.50) times crit (1, 2 or 3 times). Rewards only if the boss falls.
  • Quest: pet:quest:ID:Q sends a Mythic away for loot.

5. Own, gift, sell

  • Tie a pet to its own Counterparty token (pet:deed:ID:ASSET, 1 dander). Whoever holds the token owns the pet: gift = send the token, sell = a DEX order.

6. Cemetery and Gods

  • A pet that is never fed dies and gets a grave. Graves can be raised as zombies; a zombie can be resurrected (Resurrection) or, if it is a starving zombie, unleashed on one of your Mythics (pet:unleash:ID) to try for a Mythic God (21 Pantheon seats ever).

7. Community pet, halvings, Fire Sales

  • Every burn, and any burn that breaks a rule, feeds the shared community pet (now Homer Pepe). When it reaches Mythic it goes to Cold Storage, the next one arrives, and 6 blocks later every cost halves once more.
  • Captain Dan calls Fire Sales from lucky blocks: 6 blocks of warning, then 36 blocks at 31%.

Fair play

  • Randomness comes only from block hashes and tx ids. Burns count in chain order, once confirmed. A burn that breaks a rule is not refunded; it feeds the community pet.
  • Every new rule starts at a stated block, so older burns always replay the same.

Retired from block 974,163 (community food from then on; burns before that block keep their effect): pet:rel, pet:roll:ID (use pet:spin), pet:fa / fx / fy, pet:sk / su, pet:fl, arena modes pet:sd / tt / join, Rug Me pet:ad / rug / unad (open ads close), pet:aura (use pet:look) and pet:sign (unclaimed signs lapse).
