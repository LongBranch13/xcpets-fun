How It Works

Seeded by BitcoinEvery pet comes from a Bitcoin block, so anyone can check it and nobody can fake it.
Four rarity tiersEvery pet hatches as Common 60%, Uncommon 27%, Rare 10%, or Legendary 3%. Rarer pets get bigger stat bonuses, rarer looks and better gear. Some species only hatch at higher tiers, and Dodo is Legendary only.See every pet and its odds →
Six stagesEgg → Hatchling → Juvenile → Adult → Elder → Mythic. Each stage changes your pet's face. Gear slots open as it gets older and stays fed.
Stacking gearDress your pet in six slots, opened in this order: Headwear, Grip, Outfits, Drip, Guise and Footwear. Keep your pet fed as it gets older to unlock more slots. Every species except Nakamoto has its own ★ signature hat. Win gear, or lose it, in battles.
Burn XCPETS to playEvery extra costs XCPETS, and the tokens are burned for good. Burn to hatch a pet, then keep burning to feed it. Feed it once a day to grow it toward the next stage, and a fed, older pet unlocks more gear slots.
The community petEveryone grows one shared pet together, the Bitcoin Wizard, and every burn helps it grow.
Feed daily or it diesA pet unfed for 7 days dies and moves to the Pet Cemetery; its gear goes back to the owner's bag. Mythic pets never starve.How hunger works →
