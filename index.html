XCPETS token

The burn-to-play token on Counterparty, from an xcp.fun XCP-69 launch. Every destroy feeds the community pet; tagged burns hatch, name and grow your own pet.

Hold XCPETS in a Counterparty wallet (e.g. XCP Wallet) to play.

How it works

Seeded by BitcoinBlock hash + pet id → SHA-256 → sfc32 PRNG → traits → 32×32 pixels. Fully deterministic.
Four rarity tiersEvery pet hatches as Common 60%, Uncommon 27%, Rare 10%, or Legendary 3%. Rarer pets get bigger stat bonuses, rarer looks and better gear. Some species only hatch at higher tiers, and Dodo is Legendary only.See every pet and its odds →
Six stagesEgg → Hatchling → Juvenile → Adult → Elder → Mythic. Each stage changes the expression and adds a gear rank.
Stacking gearDress your pet in six slots: Headwear, Guise, Drip, Grip, Outfits and Footwear. Keep your pet fed as it gets older to unlock more slots. Every species has its own legendary signature hat. Win gear, or lose it, in battles.
Burn XCPETS to playEvery extra costs XCPETS, and the tokens are burned for good. Burn to hatch a pet, then keep burning to feed it. Each burn grows it toward the next stage, and a fed, older pet unlocks more gear slots.
The community petEveryone grows one shared pet together, and every burn helps it grow.
