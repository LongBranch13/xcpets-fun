Pet Guide

Thirteen meme characters can hatch from a hatch burn. Every egg rolls a rarity tier and, separately, a species. Species odds are the same in every tier: a Legendary egg is no more or less likely to be any particular pet than a Common one. All odds below come straight from the game code.

Rarity tiers

The tier is rolled on its own and does not change the species. It sets the stat bonus, and rarer tiers unlock rarer patterns, eye traits and gear rolls.

TierChanceApprox.Stat bonus
Common60%1 in 2+0
Uncommon27%1 in 4+3
Rare10%1 in 10+6
Legendary3%1 in 33+10

Example: a Legendary Dodo is 3% × 9.62% = 0.29% (about 1 in 347). Galaxy and Rainbow patterns and Laser eyes only roll on Legendary eggs.
