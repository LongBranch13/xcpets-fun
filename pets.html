Pet Guide

Thirteen meme characters can hatch from a hatch burn. From block 971,665, every egg first has a 1 in 777 chance to be Nakamoto, who is always Legendary. Otherwise it rolls one of the other twelve and, separately, a rarity tier; for those twelve, species odds are the same in every tier until block 971,669. From block 971,669 species are gated by tier again (see Species by tier), and existing pets are relabeled to the new tiers. All odds below come straight from the game code.

Species by tier (from block 971,669)

From block 971,669 the species depends on the tier again: each pet first unlocks at one tier and can hatch at that tier or any rarer one, and it is four times as likely in the tier where it unlocks. Dodo is Legendary-only, Snibby and KEVIN Rare and up, Bobo, Mumu, Sarutobi and Lenny Uncommon and up. Percentages are the chance within that tier; Overall is per egg (after the 1 in 777 Nakamoto check). Existing pets are relabeled from that block: a pet hatched earlier whose species is now below its lowest allowed tier keeps its species and looks, moves up to that tier and gains the stat bonus difference (for example an Uncommon Dodo becomes a Legendary Dodo, +7 to every stat). Pets already allowed at their tier do not change, and so does nothing before the block.

Rarity tiers

The tier is rolled first. Until block 971,669 it does not change the species; from block 971,669 it decides which species can hatch (see Species by tier). It sets the stat bonus, and rarer tiers unlock rarer patterns, eye traits and gear rolls. A Nakamoto egg is always Legendary.

Tier chances are for regular eggs; counting Nakamoto, about 3.12% of all eggs are Legendary. Galaxy and Rainbow patterns and Laser eyes only roll on Legendary eggs.

Rarest Legendary: a Legendary Nakamoto is 1 in 777. The next rarest Legendaries are KEVIN, Sarutobi and Lenny at about 1 in 661 each (0.15%); a Legendary Dodo is about 1 in 330 and a Legendary Pepe or Wojak about 1 in 275.
