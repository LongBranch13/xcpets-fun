      Gooby's No-Tail Motel

      📜 House rules & open requests

      Both parents Adult+, alive, not neglected. Two owners: you send a request (135 XCPETS); the partner's owner sees it at Gooby's desk at the motel (Gooby holds up a 💞 note; the X Circus Kekimus pending box only links here) and taps Accept within 144 blocks, which burns their 135 XCPETS with the pets flipped. The first confirmed request gets the egg. That flipped burn is the accept: that owner does not get the egg, and their pet carries the requester's best stat. Child: species 50/50, rarity usually the lower parent (fair chance higher, ~5% one tier above best), stats = average ±2, starts as an Egg with no gear/care. Parents: 1,008-block cooldown, 6 lifetime breeds each, no parent/child/sibling pairs. Breed burns feed the community pet.
